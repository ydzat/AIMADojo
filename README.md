# AIMADojo 🎬🤖

> **An AI-driven dojo for automatic MAD video creation.**

## 🧭 Motivation

AIMADojo was born out of a creator’s dilemma: how can someone with strong programming skills but limited video editing experience bring emotionally resonant MAD (Music Anime Douga) videos to life?

Traditional MAD production demands time-consuming manual editing, familiarity with tools like Adobe Premiere, and a deep understanding of visual rhythm. AIMADojo aims to break that barrier by empowering creators through AI-driven automation, allowing ideas and stories to come first — not technical constraints.

## 🧠 Vision & Idea

AIMADojo envisions an intelligent system that:
- Analyzes anime footage and extracts meaningful segments
- Understands visual-emotional content through scene labeling
- Syncs clips with music beats and user-defined narratives
- Automatically generates compelling MAD-style videos

Rather than replacing creators, AIMADojo serves as an *assistant* and *dojo* — a place to experiment, refine, and express through automation-enhanced artistry.

## 🎯 Goals

- Prototype an AI-assisted MAD generation pipeline
- Lower the barrier of entry for MAD video creation
- Preserve artistic control through flexible user guidance (story templates, emotional arcs)
- Explore the creative boundaries of AI in video storytelling

## 🔗 Relation to Other Projects

AIMADojo aligns with my broader interest in **AI-driven automation tools** and **intelligent systems that augment creativity**. It shares philosophy and infrastructure with my other projects focused on:
- Automation assistants
- Scriptable AI workflows
- Multi-modal content generation

This project may also serve as a testing ground for deeper integrations with personalized agents or operating-system-level AI interfaces in the future.
